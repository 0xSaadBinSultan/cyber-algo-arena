Before your first challenge.

Can I practice CP and CTF on the same website?

Yes. Cyber Algo Arena offers both tracks with a shared account and profile. CP solutions are judged as code; CTF solutions are checked as flags. See how the combined arena works.

Are there real testcases for the coding problems?

Yes. The built-in Packet Budget and Signal Surge problems each include two public samples and six hidden tests. The judge evaluates submitted C++, Java, or Python code against the configured tests.

Is the platform free to use?

The current arena is free to use. You can read the public guides and problem statements without an account. Sign in to submit solutions, keep your solve history, and participate in team contests.

Can a club host a combined CP and CTF contest?

Administrators can publish contests with coding problems, security challenges, or both. Teams join the contest and appear on its standings. Personal practice scores remain separate.
